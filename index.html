Before you invest

Know exactly what you are buying.

A premium experience should feel clear before the first invoice. You see the scope, responsibilities and handover terms in writing.

Clear scope

Every proposal lists included sections, content responsibilities, integrations, exclusions, revision rounds and acceptance criteria. Anything outside it is quoted before work starts.

Your business, your assets

After the agreed final payment, the handover includes the agreed website files and access details. Domain, hosting and third-party accounts should be registered in your business name wherever possible.

No fake proof

The portfolio contains clearly labelled concept work, not claimed client results. Real case studies and testimonials will be added only with client permission.

Payment

Milestones and due dates are written into the proposal. No payment is requested until scope and terms are approved.

Launch

Before launch, the agreed pages are checked for mobile layout, links, forms, metadata and basic accessibility. Third-party integrations are tested only when access is provided.

Aftercare

Any included support window, response expectations and update allowance are written down. Hosting, plugin and platform subscriptions remain separate unless expressly included.

Performance & SEO

Clean structure and sensible image handling are included as design practices; rankings, traffic, leads and perfect speed scores are never guaranteed.

Before you decide

A few useful answers.

Are these real business websites?

No. These are design concepts showing possible directions. Names, prices, addresses, imagery and sample content must be replaced with your approved business information.

Are the listed prices final?

They are illustrative starting packages. Your written proposal confirms the final scope, delivery schedule, revisions, payment stages and any third-party costs.

Will booking, payments or enquiry forms work automatically?

No. Booking, payment and CRM connections require the relevant provider and account access. Integration scope and fees are confirmed before work begins.

Who owns the website when it is paid for?

The proposal specifies the handover. The agreed custom website files and content are handed over after final payment; third-party fonts, stock assets, software and subscriptions remain subject to their own licences.

Do I have to pay everything upfront?

No payment schedule is assumed. The written proposal states the deposit, milestones and final balance before you approve the project.

Can you guarantee more customers or a first-page Google ranking?

No ethical designer can guarantee rankings or sales. The work focuses on clarity, usability, technical foundations and a smoother enquiry path; business results also depend on your offer, market, traffic and follow-up.

What do you need from me?

You provide approved business details, service and pricing information, brand assets, images you have rights to use, and timely feedback. Copywriting, photography and data entry are included only when listed in the proposal.

What happens if I need changes later?

Ongoing support and content updates are agreed in writing. New sections, features and substantial redesigns are scoped separately.
